\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We traced the collapse of unlabeled VLM adaptation on realistic batches to independent per-class parameters and replaced them by a displacement field over the vocabulary with a Gaussian Markov random field prior on a semantic class graph and an estimated class prior. \method is closed form, has anchor-only shrinkage and a global shift as extreme settings, and improves over anchored transduction where few classes are present.
