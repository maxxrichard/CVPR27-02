\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We traced the collapse of unlabeled VLM adaptation on realistic batches and streams to the independence of per-class parameters, and replaced independent prototypes by a displacement field over the class vocabulary with a Gaussian Markov random field prior on a semantic class graph and an estimated class prior. \method is closed form and gradient free, contains anchor-only shrinkage and a single global shift as extreme settings, and places absent classes at the harmonic extension of the present ones.
