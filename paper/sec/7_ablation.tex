A supplementary table compares the shared diagonal covariance with an isotropic one and with the per-class covariances of StatA, re-projection of prototypes onto the sphere, the presence pseudo-count $c$, the inherited temperature $\kappa$, and the number of outer iterations, together with the measured time per task.



\paragraph{Failure cases.}
Two failure modes are anticipated and will be reported with examples: a class whose text neighbours are visually unrelated (a polysemous name) receives a wrong displacement when absent or rare, which the $\alpha$ term bounds and the random-graph control quantifies; and a batch mixing two domains, which a single field per class cannot represent (\cref{sec:conclusion}).
