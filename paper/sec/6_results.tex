\subsection{Main comparison under realistic batches}

\subsection{Backbones and distribution shifts}
A table in the supplementary material tests the backbone-agnostic claim on OpenCLIP and SigLIP encoders and on the ImageNet shifts (low setting, $B{=}64$). Because \method uses only cosine geometry, soft counts and the class graph, no component depends on CLIP-specific logit statistics beyond the inherited temperature.



\subsection{Calibration and tail behaviour}
A supplementary table reports expected calibration error, negative log-likelihood and worst-decile accuracy in the low setting. The presence model is expected to improve calibration on batches with few classes, because it removes probability mass from classes that are not there, whereas the field is expected to improve accuracy without changing the confidence structure; both expectations are testable in the table.



\subsection{Testing the smoothness hypothesis}
\Cref{hyp:smooth} is evaluated with labels, independently of any method: for every dataset we compute the oracle displacement field and the ratio of its Dirichlet energy on $L_T$ to the mean energy over twenty degree-preserving random relabellings. A ratio well below one supports the hypothesis; a ratio near one on a dataset predicts that \method matches but does not exceed the anchor-only control there, a dataset-level prediction that \cref{tab:main} can confirm or refute. The plot is produced by the released analysis script and will be added once computed.
