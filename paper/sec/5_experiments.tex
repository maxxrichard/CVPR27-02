\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}
All experiments operate on cached features; protocol, code and configurations are released.

\subsection{Datasets and settings}
We follow the eleven-dataset suite of TransCLIP and StatA~\cite{zanella2024transclip,zanella2025stata} with the CoOp test splits~\cite{zhou2022coop}: ImageNet~\cite{deng2009imagenet}, SUN397, FGVC-Aircraft, EuroSAT, Stanford Cars, Food101, Oxford Pets, Flowers102, Caltech101, DTD and UCF101, plus the ImageNet distribution shifts ImageNet-V2~\cite{recht2019imagenetv2}, ImageNet-Sketch~\cite{wang2019sketch}, ImageNet-A~\cite{hendrycks2021imageneta} and ImageNet-R~\cite{hendrycks2021imagenetr}. Class names and prompt templates are those of CoOp and StatA. Features are extracted once per backbone and cached; no training split is used. Batch adaptation follows the seven settings of~\cite{zanella2025stata}: a task draws $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$ classes uniformly from $[1,4]$, $[2,10]$ or $[5,25]$ (very low, low, medium) with $B{=}64$ images, from $[5,25]$, $[25,50]$ or $[50,100]$ (medium, high, very high) with $B{=}1000$, or uses the full test set, with 1000 tasks per dataset. Online adaptation streams batches of 128 whose class composition follows Dirichlet($\gamma$) slot proportions, $\gamma\in\{0.1,0.01,0.001\}$ (low to high correlation) or one class after another (separate), with 100 runs per dataset.

The vocabulary sizes range from 10 (EuroSAT) to 1000 (ImageNet) and the test sets from 1,692 (DTD) to 50,000 images (ImageNet); the full list with split sizes is in the supplementary material.

\subsection{Baselines}
We compare with zero-shot CLIP, the episodic MTA~\cite{zanella2024mta}, the transductive EM-Dirichlet~\cite{martin2024transductive}, ZLaP~\cite{kalantidis2024zlap}, TransCLIP~\cite{zanella2024transclip}, StatA~\cite{zanella2025stata} and MOON~\cite{huang2026moon}, and the online TENT~\cite{wang2021tent}, TDA~\cite{karmanov2024tda}, DMN~\cite{zhang2024dmn} and OGA~\cite{fuchs2025oga}, all run with official code and default hyper-parameters through the StatA harness into which \method is plugged as a drop-in solver, so that samplers, features and prompts are identical. To attribute gains to the proposed mechanism rather than to the likelihood or the inherited terms, four architecture-matched controls share our implementation: anchor-only shrinkage ($\lambda{=}0$, the StatA/MOON mechanism in our likelihood), complete pooling (a single global shift: $\alpha{=}0$, complete graph, $\lambda\to\infty$), presence only ($\pi$ without displacement) and the unanchored mixture ($\alpha{=}\lambda{=}0$, no $\pi$).

\subsection{Implementation details}
Inherited constants follow TransCLIP and StatA and are never tuned: $\kappa{=}50$, $\lambda_y{=}1$, $\lambda_L{=}1$, $m_s{=}3$, $T_{\max}{=}10$ outer iterations with 3 inner E-step sweeps. The new constants are fixed once for all datasets and backbones: $\alpha{=}1$ (one pseudo-count, as in StatA), $\lambda{=}4$, $m{=}5$ neighbours, $\rho{=}0.05$, $c{=}\tfrac12$. The empirical Bayes variant searches $\alpha\in\{0.25,\dots,8\}$ and $\lambda\in\{0,1,\dots,32\}$ on a logarithmic grid. The linear systems are solved exactly for $K\le256$ and by 30 preconditioned conjugate-gradient iterations otherwise. Backbones: CLIP ViT-B/16 (main), ViT-L/14 and RN50~\cite{radford2021clip}, OpenCLIP ViT-B/16 trained on DataComp~\cite{cherti2023openclip}, and SigLIP ViT-B/16~\cite{zhai2023siglip}, the backbone on which~\cite{sheng2025illusion} found most TTA methods to fail. All runs use a single GPU; seeds and task indices are fixed and shared across methods. We report top-1 accuracy averaged over tasks and datasets, the worst-decile accuracy~\cite{fuchs2025oga}, expected calibration error~\cite{guo2017calibration} and time per task.
