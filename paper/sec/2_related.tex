\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Unlabeled adaptation of vision-language models.}
Transductive methods classify an unlabeled batch jointly: TransCLIP~\cite{zanella2024transclip} fits a Gaussian mixture regularised by a KL term to the zero-shot posterior and a sample-graph Laplacian, EM-Dirichlet~\cite{martin2024transductive} works on the simplex, ZLaP~\cite{kalantidis2024zlap} and ECALP~\cite{li2025ecalp} propagate labels over image and prompt graphs, and LATA~\cite{bozorgtabar2026lata} and Conf-OT~\cite{silva2025confot} use transduction for conformal prediction. Online methods adapt over a stream with feature caches (TDA~\cite{karmanov2024tda}, DMN~\cite{zhang2024dmn}, BoostAdapter~\cite{zhang2024boostadapter}), evolving prototypes (DPE~\cite{zhang2024dpe}), per-class Gaussians (OGA~\cite{fuchs2025oga}) or online proxies and label distributions (OnZeta~\cite{qian2024onzeta}, LSA~\cite{han2026lsa}); episodic methods act on single images~\cite{shu2022tpt,zanella2024mta,farina2024zero,sui2025tps}. StatA~\cite{zanella2025stata} introduced the realistic protocols with a variable number of effective classes and correlated streams, showed that these methods collapse there, and proposed shrinking each class's Gaussian parameters towards its text embedding with strength $n_k/(n_k+\alpha)$; MOON~\cite{huang2026moon} derives this shrinkage from penalised likelihood on the hypersphere and makes it confidence dependent. Both anchor every class to its own text embedding and use no information from other classes; \cref{sec:analysis} shows that their mean update is the exact $\lambda=0$ case of \method. Sheng \etal~\cite{sheng2025illusion} further report marginal gains, poor transfer to SigLIP and worse calibration, which motivates our empirical Bayes rule and backbone study.

\paragraph{Structure over classes and label shift.}
Class graphs propagate classifier weights to unseen classes in zero-shot learning~\cite{kampffmeyer2019dgp}, propagate prototypes along a category hierarchy~\cite{liu2019ppn} and select correlated classes in CLIP test-time adaptation~\cite{huang2025cosmic}; Laplacian regularisation over \emph{samples} is standard in transductive few-shot learning~\cite{ziko2020laplacianshot,zhou2004learning}. We are not aware of prior work that regularises the \emph{displacement} of VLM prototypes from their text anchors with a Gaussian Markov random field over the class graph, nor of an analysis of what such a prior does for absent classes; the modality gap literature~\cite{liang2022gap,schrodi2025two} motivates the premise that much of the displacement is shared. Adjusting a classifier to new class priors from unlabeled data is classical~\cite{saerens2002adjusting,garg2020unified}; Frolic~\cite{zhu2024frolic} corrects CLIP's label bias without labels and Chen \etal~\cite{chen2026vocab} show that open-vocabulary classifiers degrade as distractor classes are added. Our presence model is a Dirichlet-regularised estimate of the proportions inside the same objective as the prototypes.
